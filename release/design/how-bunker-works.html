How bunker turns a to-do into finished work

You choose a job on GitHub. Bunker gets an AI to do it safely, has another AI check it, tests it, and only then adds it to your project. If anything needs a person, it stops and tells you.

  • You
  • Bunker
  • AI helpers
  • GitHub
  1. Step 1 · You

    Pick a job

    Tag a to-do on GitHub so bunker knows it's ready to start.

  2. Step 2 · Bunker

    Checks it's really you

    Only people you trust can hand it work, and it won't touch a job someone changed after it was tagged.

  3. Step 3 · AI

    Does the work in a locked room

    The AI makes the change in a sealed space with no access to your passwords or your computer.

  4. Step 4 · AI

    Gets a second opinion

    A different AI reviews the change and sends it back if something isn't right.

    back to step 3 if needed
  5. Step 5 · Bunker

    Tests that it works

    Automatic tests prove the change works. If they fail, the AI gets one more go to fix it.

  6. Step 6 · GitHub

    Added to your project

    GitHub runs its own checks, then the change goes in, the job is closed and you get a note.

Sometimes · after step 5

Waits for you

If the change touches bunker's own rules or settings, it isn't added until a person approves it.

Sometimes · at any step

Couldn't do it

Bunker flags the job with the reason it stopped. When you're ready, tag it again and it starts over.

Why it's safe

  • Nothing goes in untested. The tests and GitHub's checks must pass first.
  • The AI never sees your keys. It works in a locked room, away from your passwords.
  • You stay in charge. It only works on jobs you pick, and asks before changing its own rules.
